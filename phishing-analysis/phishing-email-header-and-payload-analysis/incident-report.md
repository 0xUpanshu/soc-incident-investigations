# Incident Report – Phishing Email Investigation

## Incident Title

Phishing Email Detection and Analysis

## Date

26-Feburary-2026

---

## 1. Incident Summary

A suspicious email was analyzed to determine whether it was part of a phishing attempt. The investigation focused on sender authenticity, email content, links, and potential malicious intent.

---

## 2. Investigation Details

### Sender Analysis

* Checked sender email domain
* Verified legitimacy and possible spoofing indicators

### Email Content Review

* Examined tone, urgency, and language patterns
* Looked for impersonation or fear-based messaging

### Link Analysis

* Hovered over links to inspect actual URLs
* Checked for mismatched domains or suspicious redirects

### Attachment Review

* Reviewed attachment type and naming pattern
* Evaluated risk based on file extension and context

---

## 3. Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)

* Suspicious sender domain
* Potential phishing link
* Social engineering language

---

## 4. MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

* Initial Access – Phishing

---

## 5. Verdict

Phishing / Suspicious / Legitimate (select based on findings)

---

## 6. Risk Impact

* Possible credential theft
* Unauthorized account access
* User compromise risk

---

## 7. Recommendations

* Block sender/domain if malicious
* Educate users about phishing awareness
* Enable email filtering and monitoring
* Report similar emails to security team

---

## 8. Lessons Learned

This investigation improved understanding of phishing detection techniques and SOC-level analysis workflows.
