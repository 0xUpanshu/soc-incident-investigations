# Phishing Email Lab – Summary

## Lab Overview

This lab focuses on analyzing a suspected phishing email and identifying indicators that suggest malicious intent. The objective is to understand how phishing attempts are structured and how a SOC analyst evaluates such emails during real investigations.

## Objective

* Identify whether the email is legitimate or malicious
* Analyze sender details, links, and attachments
* Detect social engineering techniques used in the email
* Understand how phishing campaigns attempt to trick users

## Key Observations

* The email content was designed to create urgency and prompt immediate action.
* Sender information appeared suspicious and required validation.
* Links and/or attachments were inspected for legitimacy.
* The language and structure of the email suggested possible impersonation.

## Skills Practiced

* Email threat analysis
* Identifying phishing indicators
* Recognizing social engineering patterns
* Investigative thinking in a SOC environment

## Learning Outcome

This lab helped in understanding how phishing attacks operate and how analysts verify the authenticity of emails using a structured investigation approach.
