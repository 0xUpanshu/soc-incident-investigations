# Incident Report – Introduction to SIEM

## Incident Overview
A SIEM-generated alert was reviewed as part of this lab to understand the investigation and validation process followed by SOC analysts.

---

## Alert Information
- **Alert Type:** General Suspicious Activity
- **Severity:** Informational / Low
- **Detection Source:** SIEM correlation logic
- **Environment:** Simulated lab environment

---

## Investigation Summary
The alert was examined to identify indicators of malicious activity.  
Key aspects reviewed during the investigation included:
- Event context
- Source and destination details
- Frequency and timing of events
- Any abnormal or repeated behavior

---

## Findings
- The alert was generated based on predefined detection logic
- No clear indicators of compromise were identified
- Activity observed was consistent with expected or benign behavior
- No escalation criteria were met

---

## Conclusion
**Incident Classification:** False Positive  

The alert did not represent a confirmed security incident.  
No remediation or containment actions were required.

---

## Lessons Learned
- SIEM alerts require analyst validation before escalation
- Context and correlation are essential for accurate incident classification
- Proper triage helps reduce alert fatigue in SOC operations
