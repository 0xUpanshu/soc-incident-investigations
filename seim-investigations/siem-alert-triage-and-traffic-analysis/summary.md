# Lab Summary – Introduction to SIEM

This lab introduced the core concepts behind SIEM platforms and how they are used in real-world SOC operations.

## Key Takeaways
- SIEM platforms centralize logs from multiple sources into a single system
- Raw logs are treated as **events**
- Detection rules convert events into **alerts**
- Alerts require analyst investigation before being classified as **incidents**
- Not all alerts indicate malicious activity; false positives are common

## SOC Perspective
From a SOC analyst’s point of view, SIEM tools are not decision-makers but decision-support systems.  
Human analysis and context are required to confirm whether an alert represents a real security threat.

## Overall Learning
This lab reinforced the importance of structured alert review and highlighted why investigation and validation are critical steps in incident handling.
