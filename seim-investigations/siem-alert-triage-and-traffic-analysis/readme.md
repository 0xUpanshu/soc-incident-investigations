# Introduction to SIEM – TryHackMe

## Overview
This lab covers the fundamentals of **Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)** and its role within a Security Operations Center (SOC).  
The focus is on understanding how logs are collected, correlated, and analyzed to support security monitoring and incident detection.

---

## Objectives
- Understand the purpose of a SIEM in a SOC environment
- Learn the difference between events, alerts, and incidents
- Identify common log sources ingested by SIEM platforms
- Recognize the importance of analyst validation and false positives

---

## Skills Demonstrated
- SIEM fundamentals
- Log analysis concepts
- SOC analyst mindset
- Incident identification logic

---

## Outcome
After completing this lab, I developed a clear understanding of how SIEM platforms assist SOC analysts in detecting, investigating, and validating potential security incidents.
